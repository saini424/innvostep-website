## Build Before Everyone Else
The New Era Belongs To Student Entrepreneurs. Most students are preparing for placements, but a small group is preparing for ownership. That small difference changes everything. 

**Why wait?** A teenager can build an audience from a hostel room and launch a startup using AI tools today.

### The Success Blueprint
- **Building audiences**: Your network is your net worth.
- **Creating startups**: Turn problems into products.
- **Learning AI**: Tools that allow you to work at 10x speed.
- **Selling online**: The skill that pays forever.

## The Biggest Myth: "I Need Money"
This is the biggest lie students believe. Modern entrepreneurship is not about huge investments. You can start with:
- **A phone**
- **A laptop**
- **Internet connection**
- **Free tools** (Canva, ChatGPT, Notion)

### Key Action Areas
1. **Marketing**: Learn how to tell a story people want to hear.
2. **Communication**: Sell your vision to partners and customers.
3. **Execution**: Ideas are cheap. Shipping products is what matters.
