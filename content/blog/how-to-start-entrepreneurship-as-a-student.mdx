# 🧠 Entrepreneurial Thinking Changes Everything

Entrepreneurship starts with mindset.

Most people consume.

Entrepreneurs observe.

Most people scroll.

Entrepreneurs analyze.

Every successful startup begins from:
- A problem
- A frustration
- A market gap
- An inefficiency

Start noticing things around you.

Ask yourself:
- Why is this slow?
- Why do people complain?
- What solution is missing?
- What can be improved?
- Why does this system fail?

That is how entrepreneurial thinking develops.
